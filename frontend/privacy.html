🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Your privacy matters. PresetHub uses personal information only where reasonably needed to operate accounts, deliver purchases/downloads, support creators, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. We do not sell or rent personal information. No website can promise that data will never be exposed under every possible circumstance; we work to reduce that risk through access controls, encryption and security monitoring.

गोपनीयता नीति — हिंदी में संक्षेप

PresetHub आपका नाम, ईमेल, प्रोफाइल/पोस्ट, वेबसाइट उपयोग से जुड़ी तकनीकी जानकारी और खरीद/भुगतान का रिकॉर्ड सेवा चलाने, खरीद पूरी करने, सुरक्षा और सहायता के लिए उपयोग करता है। हम आपकी निजी जानकारी बेचते या किराये पर नहीं देते।

  • आपका पासवर्ड plain text में नहीं रखा जाता; payout के बैंक/UPI विवरण database में encrypted रहते हैं और सामान्य जगहों पर masked दिखते हैं।
  • भुगतान Cashfree, PhonePe या YouPI जैसे चुने गए payment provider से होता है। PresetHub आपका पूरा card number, CVV या UPI PIN नहीं मांगता/रखता।
  • आपकी private जानकारी दूसरे users को सार्वजनिक नहीं दिखाई जाती। Payout जांचने के लिए केवल अधिकृत admin को जरूरत के अनुसार विवरण दिख सकता है और access log किया जाता है।
  • हम सुरक्षा के लिए HTTPS, password hashing, encryption, access controls और monitoring जैसे उपाय अपनाते हैं। फिर भी internet पर 100% सुरक्षा की गारंटी कोई नहीं दे सकता।
  • आप अपने registered email से support@presethub.site पर जानकारी देखने, सुधारने या हटाने का अनुरोध कर सकते हैं। कानूनी/लेन-देन संबंधी रिकॉर्ड जरूरी अवधि तक रखे जा सकते हैं।

हम आपकी जानकारी की सुरक्षा को गंभीरता से लेते हैं। यदि कोई reportable data breach पता चलता है, तो हम जांच, रोकथाम और लागू कानून के अनुसार प्रभावित users/authorities को सूचना देने की कार्रवाई करेंगे।

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: name, email, username, password hash and account/security status. We do not store your plain-text password.
  • Profile and content: avatar, bio, social links, presets, reviews, comments, messages or support requests you choose to submit.
  • Usage and technical data: pages and presets viewed, searches, downloads, timestamps, IP address, browser/device information, logs and cookie preferences. This helps operate and secure the service.
  • Payment records: order amount, status, provider reference and subscription/purchase history. Payments are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout; PresetHub does not store full card numbers, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Creator payout details: if you request creator earnings, you may provide a bank account or UPI ID. These payout details are encrypted in our database and displayed in masked form in normal account views.

2. Why We Use Information

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate sessions and respond to support requests.
  • Process purchases, subscriptions, preset downloads, refunds and creator payouts.
  • Show creator profiles, attribute sales/downloads and calculate creator earnings.
  • Detect fraud, suspicious payments, spam, abuse and technical failures.
  • Improve site performance, reliability, accessibility and user experience.
  • Send essential account, payment, security and service notifications. Promotional communications are limited to applicable consent and settings.

3. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only when needed:

  • Payment providers: Cashfree, PhonePe and/or YouPI, depending on the checkout route enabled for a transaction. Their own privacy terms also apply.
  • Hosting and infrastructure: hosting, database, file storage, email, analytics, security and delivery providers that help run PresetHub.
  • Public creator content: information and content you choose to publish publicly (such as username, profile, presets and posts) can be viewed by other visitors.
  • Legal and safety: competent authorities or relevant parties when disclosure is legally required, necessary to protect users/platform rights, or needed to investigate fraud or security incidents.

4. Who Can See Your Private Information?

Your password is stored as a one-way hash, not as readable text. Bank/UPI payout details are encrypted at rest and are not shown in public profiles. Only authorised personnel who need the information to review and process a payout may access the full payout destination. Such access is restricted and logged. We do not intentionally expose private account, payment or payout details to other users.

5. Security and Data Protection

We use safeguards appropriate to the type of information, which may include HTTPS in transit, password hashing, encrypted payout fields, restricted administrative access, session/CSRF protections, rate limits, backups and security/error monitoring. Access is limited to operational needs. We regularly review and improve these safeguards.

Important: no internet transmission, storage system or service can be guaranteed 100% secure. We cannot truthfully promise that a breach or unauthorised access is impossible. If we become aware of a reportable personal-data breach, we will investigate, take steps to contain and remediate it, and provide notices to affected users or authorities where required by applicable law.

6. Cookies, Local Storage and Advertising

Essential cookies and browser storage support sign-in, CSRF protection, preferences and site functionality. Optional advertising technology is not loaded until you choose Allow optional cookies in the cookie consent banner. If you reject optional cookies, PresetHub does not intentionally load the Google AdSense advertising script. You can change your choice using the Cookie settings control. Blocking or clearing cookies may sign you out or affect features.

7. Data Retention

We keep account and service data while it is needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, preserve transaction records and comply with legal obligations. Some financial, fraud-prevention or audit records may need to be retained after account closure for the period required by law or legitimate operational needs.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification and legal/transactional retention requirements. You can update profile information and cookie choices through available site controls. Account deletion may not immediately erase records that must be retained for payments, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

For privacy requests, contact support@presethub.site from your registered email and describe the request. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, CVVs or UPI PINs.

9. Children's Privacy

PresetHub is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. International Processing

Our infrastructure or service providers may process information in locations outside your state or country. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure appropriate safeguards for such processing.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this policy when our features, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the service where appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy or data-protection questions, contact support@presethub.site.
