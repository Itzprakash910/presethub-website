Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

PresetHub ("we", "us") runs a marketplace for Lightroom presets and shayari at presethub.site. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules.

1. Data we collect and why

  • Account (sign-in by Clerk): email address, name, and profile picture from your sign-in. Passwords are handled by Clerk and are never stored by PresetHub. Purpose: to create and secure your account and let you buy, download, post and earn.
  • Profile: username, bio, and optional mobile number (needed for payments through Cashfree). Purpose: your public profile and payment processing.
  • Orders and payouts: purchases, coupon use, tips, boosts, and (for creators) payout method details, stored encrypted; the screen shows masked details only. Purpose: to deliver what you bought and to pay creators.
  • Content you post: presets, shayari, comments, reports. Purpose: to run the marketplace.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and security logs. Purpose: security, fraud prevention, and fixing problems.
  • Analytics and advertising (only with your consent): Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel measure visits and ads. They load only after you accept cookies. You can change this at any time from the cookie banner or the cookie policy page.

2. Who we share data with

  • Clerk (sign-in and account security).
  • Cashfree (payment processing). We do not store card or bank details.
  • Hosting and database providers (Render, MongoDB Atlas) for storage.
  • Resend (sending account and order emails).
  • Google and Meta, only if you accept analytics and advertising cookies.
  • Telegram, only if you link your account to the PresetHub bot.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. How long we keep data

  • Account data: while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile is removed and your sign-in is deleted from Clerk.
  • Orders, payouts and tax records: kept for as long as the law requires (accounting and tax), even after account deletion. Personal details are removed where the law allows.
  • Security and audit logs: kept for one year.
  • Analytics: according to the retention set in Google Analytics (we use 14 months).

4. Your rights

  • Access: download a copy of your data from your account (Profile → "Download my data"), or request it by email.
  • Correction: edit your name, username and bio in your profile. For email changes, contact us.
  • Erasure: delete your account from your profile. Some records are kept only where the law requires it.
  • Withdraw consent: change your cookie choice at any time. Withdrawing does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Nominate: you may nominate another person to exercise your rights if you become unable to do so.

To use any right, email us at support@presethub.site from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act.

5. Grievance officer and complaints

  • Grievance officer: [Name of designated person], support@presethub.site, [postal address]
  • How we handle complaints: we acknowledge your complaint, investigate it, and reply in writing within the time set by the DPDP Rules.
  • Unresolved complaints: if you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Children

PresetHub is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 and we do not show behavioural advertising to them. If we learn that we have data from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it. If you believe a child has given us data, contact support@presethub.site.

7. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, access controls for admin staff (with role-based permissions and two-step verification), encryption for payout details, audit logs for admin actions, and daily database backups. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay and report it as the law requires.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you by email or in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@presethub.site
