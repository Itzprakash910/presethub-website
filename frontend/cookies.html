Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

PresetHub uses limited browser storage and cookies to keep the marketplace functional and to remember privacy choices.

1. Essential cookies

The ph_auth cookie keeps an authenticated session active. It is HttpOnly, uses SameSite protection and is not used for advertising. A CSRF cookie named ph_csrf is readable by the site JavaScript so state-changing requests can include a matching CSRF header.

2. Optional advertising

Google AdSense is loaded only after you choose Allow optional cookies. If you reject optional cookies, the advertising script is not intentionally loaded by PresetHub. Google may set additional cookies when advertising is enabled; those technologies are subject to Google's own policies and controls.

3. Managing your choice

Use the Cookie settings button on public pages to reopen the consent prompt. Clearing browser storage also resets the local consent choice.

4. More information

For broader information about account, payment, content and privacy practices, see our Privacy Policy and Terms.
