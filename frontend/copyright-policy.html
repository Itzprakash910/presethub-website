Copyright and Takedown Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

PresetHub is a platform where creators share Lightroom presets and text posts. We respect the intellectual property of others and expect our users to do the same.

1. What creators must confirm

  • You may upload only presets, images and text that you created or that you have a written right to share.
  • Do not upload presets that were bought, leaked or copied from another creator, or that contain someone else's photograph without permission.
  • Preview images must show your own work or work you are licensed to show.

2. How to report an infringement

If you believe content on PresetHub infringes your copyright or other rights, email the grievance officer below with:

  • A description of the work you own and, where possible, a link to the original.
  • The exact PresetHub page or preset link you want reviewed.
  • Your name, address, email and phone number.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your signature (typed name is accepted).

Sending a notice you know to be false can have legal consequences. We may share a notice with the uploader so they can respond.

3. What we do

  • We review valid notices promptly. If the claim looks valid we remove or disable access to the content and tell the uploader.
  • We follow the notice-and-takedown approach of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, and act on valid notices under the US DMCA where it applies.
  • Accounts that repeatedly infringe are suspended or removed.

4. Counter-notice

If your content was removed by mistake, email the grievance officer with the link, your contact details and a statement that you believe the content was removed in error and that you own it or have permission. If the person who complained does not take legal action within a reasonable period, we may restore the content.

5. Other rights

The same process applies to trademarks, personal photographs and privacy complaints. Reports about abuse or illegal content can also be made with the Report button on any preset or post.

Contact

  • Grievance officer: PresetHub Grievance Officer, support@presethub.site
  • We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
