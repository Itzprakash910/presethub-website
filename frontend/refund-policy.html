Refund, Cancellation and Shipping Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

PresetHub sells digital products (Lightroom presets, shayari access and Pro memberships). Nothing is shipped physically.

1. Shipping and delivery

Presets are delivered instantly as a download after successful payment. There is no physical shipping.

2. Refunds for presets

  • If you were charged but the preset did not download because of a technical fault on our side, contact us within 7 days and we will fix the download or refund you.
  • If the preset is materially different from its description, contact us within 7 days with the details and we will review it.
  • Presets that you have already downloaded are otherwise not refundable, because they are digital and cannot be returned.
  • Duplicate charges for the same preset are always refunded.

3. Pro memberships

  • Pro is a prepaid plan (monthly, 3-monthly or yearly). It does not renew automatically.
  • If you were charged twice or the payment failed after the plan was activated, we refund the extra amount.
  • Unused time is not refunded once a plan has started, except where the law requires it.

4. Tips and boosts

  • Tips to creators are final, except duplicate or failed payments, which are refunded.
  • Boosts are refunded only if the boost was not applied due to a fault on our side.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Cashfree. Bank processing can take 5 to 7 working days after approval. If a creator was already paid for a sale that is refunded, the creator's balance is adjusted.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email support@presethub.site with your order ID (shown in your order list), the email on your account, and the reason. We reply within 3 working days.

7. Cancellation

You can stop using PresetHub at any time. To delete your account, use the account settings. Pro memberships end on their expiry date and do not renew.

8. Seller information

  • Legal name of business: Details are available on request at support@presethub.site
  • Registered address: Available on request at support@presethub.site
  • Contact: support@presethub.site
  • Grievance officer: PresetHub Grievance Officer, support@presethub.site
