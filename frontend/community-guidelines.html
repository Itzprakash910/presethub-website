Community Guidelines

Last updated: 15 November 2026

PresetHub is a friendly place for photographers and creators. These rules keep it safe and useful. They apply to presets, previews, profiles, comments, reviews, messages and text posts.

1. Not allowed

  • Illegal content, or content that encourages violence, self-harm or crime.
  • Any sexual content involving minors (reported to the authorities), and nudity or sexually explicit material.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, bullying or sharing someone's private information.
  • Presets or files that contain malware, scripts or links that harm devices or steal data.
  • Stolen, leaked, resold or copied presets (see the Copyright and Takedown Policy).
  • Spam, fake reviews, fake followers, misleading titles or preview images that do not match the preset.
  • Attempts to hack, overload or scrape the website, or to get around payments, limits or security.

2. Uploading

  • Upload only files you own. Use clear names, honest previews and correct categories.
  • Supported preset formats and size limits are shown on the upload form. Files that do not match their format are rejected.
  • Paid presets must deliver what the description promises.

3. Reporting

Use the Report button on a preset, comment or profile, or contact us. Reports are reviewed by our team and treated confidentially.

4. What happens if rules are broken

  • We may remove content, warn you, limit features, suspend or permanently remove your account.
  • Serious or illegal violations may be reported to the authorities.
  • If you think we made a mistake, write to us and we will review it again.

5. Security

If you find a security problem, please report it privately as described in our security.txt instead of posting it publicly.

Contact

  • Grievance officer: PresetHub Grievance Officer, support@presethub.site
  • We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
